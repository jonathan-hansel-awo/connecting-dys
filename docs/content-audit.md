## Editorial choices

Homepage language about what connection _can_ mean is editorial copy, never attributed to a member. No fabricated quotations, statistics, portraits, staff credentials, partner logos or event dates appear. The community-voices chapter is omitted. Original artwork is abstract and decorative, with no claims that it depicts members or represents universal traits of any learning difference.

The site describes a Community Interest Company. It does not solicit donations or describe a registered charity. No clinical service or diagnosis is offered.
